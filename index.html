The story

Technology should expand agency.

Born in New York City and raised in the South Bronx and Mount Vernon, I learned to move between neighborhood reality and institutional systems. My path took me from financial advising and managing approximately $81 million in assets to cryptocurrency, Web3 development and building AI systems.

Today I connect technology, culture and commerce so founders, creators and emerging talent can build lasting capability and ownership.
